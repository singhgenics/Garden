Name sheet · decision made

Cabin East stays.

This page is for whoever finishes the signage, menu and merch. It says what the name is, how it is written and said, and what to check before anything is printed.

The nameCabin EastWritten CABIN EAST in capitals on signs and merch, Cabin East in running text.
The nicknameEastyWhat regulars and staff say. Informal only, never on the licence.
The descriptorPan-Asian Bar & KitchenSits under the name on the sign, menu cover and website header.
The taglineWOK | STEAM | DRAUGHTWok for craft, steam for comfort, draught for company. Happy hour 12 to 6.

Where each one goes

Sign and gate standeeLogo with tagline. The standee still needs the name added now it is final.
Licences and billsCabin East only. It must match the FSSAI licence and GST exactly.
Menu coverLogo, then “Pan-Asian Bar & Kitchen”, then the tagline.
Social bios“Cabin East · Pan-Asian Bar & Kitchen · a garden by the road.” Say Easty in captions.
Staff and regulars“See you at Easty.”
The roomCabin = the open hall. Garden = its backyard. The brass CE monogram stays on the entrance column.

Voice, in four rules

  • Short and warm. Seven words or fewer.
  • One joke per piece, never the same joke twice.
  • Use the menu names for the jokes: Cabin Fever, Bao Down, Noodle Around.
  • Joke about the food and ourselves, never about the guest.
