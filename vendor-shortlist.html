Every entry comes from public web search results and directory listings, not from a call. I could not open IndiaMART, bizgoa, ExportersIndia or the Goa Industrial Promotion Board site from this environment, so there are no phone numbers, prices or minimum orders, and no listing is confirmed to be still trading. Category 1

Seafood

Menu and stock it feeds: Tiger prawns 4 kg/day, chonak fillet 0.8 kg/day: Prawn to Be Wild, Salt & Pepa, Noodle Around mixed, Green With Envy prawn

Note
Chonak (Indian salmon) can be scarce and costly. Ask for kingfish or a firm white fish as the standing substitute, and have the chef decide before you sign a rate.

Margao wholesale fish market (SGPDA)

South GoaVerify by phone

Goa’s only wholesale fish market; restaurants buy here.

Why: Best price and range for prawns and chonak. Closest wholesale point to Cavelossim.

Ask: Where is trading happening now (the redeveloped complex is not fully open)? Which traders sell to restaurants and will deliver or hold an order for a morning pickup?

Menu and stock it feeds: Onion, potato, garlic, ginger, capsicum, cabbage, carrot, mushrooms, lemongrass, galangal, Thai basil, bok choy, green and bird’s eye chilli, limes, green papaya, spring onion, coriander

Note
No supplier in Goa was found for bok choy, Thai basil or kaffir lime leaves. Test Hyperpure and Abselut first. Otherwise plan a weekly courier from Bengaluru or Mumbai (kaffir lime keeps 12 months frozen, per the stock plan).

Margao wholesale fruit and vegetable trade

South GoaVerify by phone

The biggest wholesale produce trade in Goa, running from about 4 am to 8.30 pm around Gandhi and New Market. Hinterland farmers from Quepem and Canacona also sell here.

Why: Cheapest for the basics, and the local farmers are a natural source for green papaya, chilli and limes.

Ask: Pick two stall holders for a standing order and daily or alternate-day delivery. Pricing changes daily.
