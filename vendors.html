Step 0

Licence first

Read this before you call anyone for alcohol
Goa Excise says a wholesaler may sell only to a holder of a valid retail licence (packed bottles, drunk on the premises) in Goa. Until the bar licence is issued, a wholesaler or brewery cannot legally sell us beer, spirits or feni. Food and soft-drink vendors have no such rule.

You can still talk to the breweries and wholesalers now, compare prices and agree terms for the day the licence arrives.

Source: Goa Excise, wholesale of IMFL. Licence codes and fees differ between websites, so confirm them with the Excise office or your advocate. The official site did not open when this was researched.

Ask each brewery

  • Do you deliver kegs to Cavelossim, and what do you charge for delivery?
  • Price per litre, keg sizes, and the keg deposit (money held until the empty keg comes back).
  • Who supplies the CO2 gas bottle and the tap fittings, and who fixes them if they fail?
  • How long does a keg stay fresh once tapped, and how cold must it be kept?
  • Can you supply a guest tap that changes every two weeks?
  • Do you need our excise licence number before the first order?
Section 2

Spirits and feni

Find by phone

Feni: cashew and coconut

Small distillers sell feni in bulk to bottlers and straight to bars. Coconut feni is mostly made and drunk in South Goa, which suits the coconut highball. Use sealed, labelled bottles from a distiller or bottler on the state list. Fake liquor is a known problem in Goa.

State list of feni bottling plants: goaipb.goa.gov.in (PDF). It did not open when this was researched, so open it yourself and pick two bottlers near Cavelossim.

Not found

Whisky, vodka, agave, gin, soju

These come from a licensed wholesaler of Indian-made foreign liquor (IMFL) once the licence is in hand. No wholesaler was named online. Ask the Excise office for the list of licensed wholesalers, then get at least two quotes.

Check each brand on our menu is available in Goa: Paul John, Glenlivet 12, Talisker 10, Dewar’s White Label, Jameson, Suntory Toki, and a soju for Soju Yakult and Somaek.
