Work plan

What to do, in what order: first the kitchen, store room, dish wash and washroom, then the restaurant, then the outdoor area. Built from the 3D model, the equipment plan and the architect sheets. 88 tasks.

The priority list

Priority 1

Kitchen, store room, dish wash, washroom

Nothing opens without it: the licences, the hygiene rules and the first cook all depend on this block. It also holds the longest-lead items (drain outfall, roof fan and duct, refrigeration, tests, water report) and the dust and noise, so it goes first, before the hall is dressed.

Priority 2

Restaurant: hall, bar, coffee wall, pool lounge

Starts its dirty work (roof, edges, brick, tile) once the back of house is under cover; dressing waits for the deep clean. Lighting and furniture come last so they are not covered in dust.

Priority 3

Outdoor: gate, path, landing, garden

Last, so delivery lorries and tradesmen do not wreck the path and plants. Only the service yard (gas cage, grease-trap access, bins) is done early.

Durations are my planning estimates in working days, not vendor quotes: replace them as quotes arrive.

Critical path (back of house): Outfall found (0.2) → drains and trap (2.1–2.3) → floor to falls (2.4) → flood test (2.5) → wall and floor finishes (5.1) → hood and ranges (6.1) → gas, hood and drainage tests (7.1–7.5) → deep clean (8.1) → stock in (8.3). The roof fan and duct (3.1, 4.1) run beside it and must finish before the hood goes in.

Before you start: open questions

None of these is verified. Each one is a task in the plan below; do them first.

  • Outfall. Where the drain goes is not known (sewer, septic tank or soak pit). Everything underground waits for this (task 0.2).
  • Store door vs rack. The store door moved to 2.65 to 3.55 m from the west wall and now opens against SR-W1: shorten or move the rack (6.5).
  • Dish-wash bowls. Plan says two, three other pages say three (0.6).
  • Structure. The temporary wall, the 500 L tank bracket and the roof slab are unchecked (0.3).
  • Fans and ACs. The listing gives no sweep, depth or power for the 17 fans and two ACs (R2.2).
  • Pool lounge. Depth is calculated (2.48 m), not measured; 6 ft table is a tight fit (R0.4).
