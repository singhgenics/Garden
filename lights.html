Lights through the day

Every light in the 3D model, and how bright it should be at each point of the day. Numbers are the dimmer setting (100 = full). They match the model's Lighting presets (Lunch, Sunset, Evening, Late), so you can preview each one there.

Wire it in five groups. A: table orbs. B: wall lights (gallery spots, bell lamps, picture lights, brick-bay lanterns). C: festoon on the beams. D: bar. E: outdoor (path, tree, gate sign) on a timer with a light sensor. A to D each on its own dimmer, so one person can set the whole room in four turns of a knob.
How bright is right. Tables at dinner: about 50–80 lux, a warm pool on each table and no bare bulb in a seated guest's eye line. Bar counter: about 150 lux. Kitchen and dish wash: 500 lux, cool white, never dimmed. Washroom: about 200 lux. All dining bulbs 2200 K so the room glows amber; walls and art 2700 K.
Rules of thumb. Dim in steps, not all at once: change the room every hour after sunset. Late at night keep the walls and the bar a little brighter than the tables: the room still looks full. On a dark monsoon afternoon, use the Sunset settings at lunch. After close, turn everything up to full to clean, then off.
