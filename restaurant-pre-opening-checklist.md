# Restaurant Pre-Opening Checklist

Timeline: start 6–12 months out. Tick items off as you go. Written for a restaurant in Goa, India (Cavelossim, Salcete). Items tagged 🇮🇳 are India/Goa-specific. Rules, fees and thresholds change, so confirm each one with the authority or a local consultant before relying on it.

## 1. Concept, Business Plan & Finance (12+ months out)

- [ ] Concept, cuisine, target customer, price point and USP defined
- [ ] Market and competitor research; catchment analysis (tourists vs locals, season vs off-season)
- [ ] 🇮🇳 Opening date planned around Goa's season (peak roughly Oct–May); fit-out and roof/drainage work finished before the monsoon (June–Sept)
- [ ] Business plan: startup budget, 12–24 month P&L, cash flow, break-even, with a lean-monsoon cash plan
- [ ] Contingency fund (10–20% of budget) and 3–6 months working capital
- [ ] Funding secured (equity, loans, investors); current account opened
- [ ] CA/accountant appointed; accounting and GST bookkeeping set up
- [ ] Target food cost %, labour cost %, prime cost % and rent % set

## 2. Legal, Licensing & Compliance (9–12 months out)

> Status: all licences and approvals are complete.

- [x] Business structure chosen and registered (proprietorship, partnership/LLP, or Pvt Ltd); PAN obtained; Udyam (MSME) registration optional
- [x] 🇮🇳 Land-use and property documents verified: title, conversion (sanad) status, TCP zoning allows commercial/restaurant use, occupancy/completion certificate
- [x] 🇮🇳 CRZ check (Cavelossim is coastal): confirm distance from High Tide Line, No Development Zone rules, and any Goa Coastal Zone Management Authority permission
- [x] 🇮🇳 Village Panchayat (Cavelossim) construction/renovation permission and NOC for the restaurant use
- [x] 🇮🇳 Trade licence from the Village Panchayat (renewed annually)
- [x] 🇮🇳 FSSAI licence via FoSCoS (State licence or Registration depending on turnover; applied under the right kitchen category) with the licence number displayed and printed on bills/menus
- [x] 🇮🇳 FSSAI Food Safety Supervisor (FoSTaC-trained) appointed; Food Safety Display Board put up
- [x] 🇮🇳 Fire safety NOC from the Goa Directorate of Fire & Emergency Services
- [x] 🇮🇳 Goa State Pollution Control Board consent (Consent to Establish/Operate) if applicable; DG-set and noise norms
- [x] 🇮🇳 Goa Excise licence for serving liquor/beer (bar licence, draught beer, display of licence and price list; know the dry days and age limit)
- [x] 🇮🇳 Goa Tourism trade registration (Goa Registration of Tourist Trade Act) if applicable to your premises
- [x] 🇮🇳 Shops & Establishments registration (Goa Labour Dept) and Goa Professional Tax registration
- [x] 🇮🇳 GST registration (confirm rate and input-tax-credit choice with your CA); FSSAI number and GSTIN on invoices
- [x] 🇮🇳 EPF and ESI registration once headcount thresholds are met; gratuity and minimum-wage compliance for Goa
- [x] 🇮🇳 Legal Metrology registration/verification for weighing scales and any packaged goods
- [x] 🇮🇳 Music licences: IPRS and PPL (copyright) for recorded music, plus police/panchayat permission for live music or loudspeakers; observe the noise-hours rules
- [x] 🇮🇳 Commercial electricity connection (Goa Electricity Department), water connection (PWD) or borewell approval, commercial LPG connection
- [x] 🇮🇳 Waste-management arrangement with the panchayat or an authorised collector; follow Goa's single-use plastic restrictions
- [x] Trademark registration for the name/logo (IP India)
- [x] Lease or ownership documents reviewed by a lawyer (term, fit-out period, rent-free period, exit clauses)
- [x] Insurance: public liability, fire and property, business interruption, workers' compensation, product liability
- [x] Contracts: supplier, landlord, employment, Zomato/Swiggy, POS/software agreements
- [x] Privacy policy for guest and staff data

## 3. Location, Design & Construction (6–9 months out)

- [ ] Site selected; footfall, visibility, access, parking, delivery access verified
- [ ] Architect/interior designer/MEP consultant appointed
- [ ] Floor plan: kitchen flow (receiving → storage → prep → cook → pass → wash), dining layout, seat count, accessibility
- [ ] Utilities capacity confirmed: power (3-phase), LPG, water, drainage, ventilation, internet
- [ ] 🇮🇳 Power backup: inverter or DG set sized for kitchen, chillers and lights (Goa has monsoon outages); surge protection
- [ ] 🇮🇳 Water: storage tank, borewell/PWD supply, filtration/RO; potable-water test report from an NABL lab (FSSAI requirement)
- [ ] 🇮🇳 Wastewater: septic tank/soak pit or STP as per panchayat/GSPCB rules, grease trap, floor drains sloped for heavy rain
- [ ] 🇮🇳 Open-sided hall protected for monsoon: roof, gutters, PVC curtains/blinds, anti-slip floor, damp-proofing, termite treatment
- [ ] Kitchen extraction/hood and fire suppression designed; gas leak detection
- [ ] Contractor appointed; fit-out programme and budget agreed
- [ ] 🇮🇳 FSSAI Schedule 4 layout points met: washable surfaces, fly-proofing, hand-wash stations, separate raw and cooked areas, covered bins
- [ ] Toilets (guest and staff), changing area, staff rest area, dry store, cold storage, waste area
- [ ] 🇮🇳 Staff accommodation (staff room) meets basic standards: ventilation, fire exit, wiring, water and toilets
- [ ] Snagging list completed and signed off; handover documents and warranties collected
- [ ] Deep clean after construction before any food arrives

## 4. Menu & Food Development (4–6 months out)
- [ ] Menu engineered for profitability, kitchen capacity and skill level
- [ ] Every recipe standardised with written spec, yield and photo
- [ ] Each dish costed; selling price and margin set
- [ ] Allergen matrix and dietary labelling
- [ ] 🇮🇳 Veg (green dot) / non-veg (brown dot) marking on every dish as FSSAI requires; beef/pork and Goan-specific items labelled clearly
- [ ] Menu tastings with team; menu tweaked after trial service
- [ ] Beverage, dessert, kids and takeaway menus finalised
- [ ] Menu design, print and digital (QR) versions proofread for spelling, prices, allergens
- [ ] Specials/seasonal menu process defined

## 5. Suppliers, Purchasing & Inventory (3–4 months out)
- [ ] Primary and backup suppliers for each category (meat, fish, produce, dairy, dry goods, beverages, packaging, cleaning)
- [ ] 🇮🇳 Supplier approval: FSSAI licence numbers on file, cold-chain, samples, references; liquor bought only from licensed Goa wholesalers
- [ ] Price lists, credit terms, delivery days, minimums, substitution policy agreed in writing
- [ ] Par levels and reorder points set
- [ ] Opening stock order placed and delivery timed 2–3 days before soft opening
- [ ] Receiving procedure: temperature checks, date checks, rejection process
- [ ] Storage system: FIFO, labelling, date-coding, correct shelf order (raw below ready-to-eat)
- [ ] Inventory/stocktake template and count schedule set

## 6. Equipment, Smallwares & Furniture (3–4 months out)
- [ ] Kitchen equipment ordered, delivered, installed and tested (ovens, ranges, fryers, grills, combi, refrigeration, freezers, blast chiller, dishwasher, prep tables, extraction)
- [ ] Service and maintenance contracts and warranties arranged
- [ ] Smallwares: pots, pans, knives, boards (colour-coded), containers, thermometers, scales, utensils
- [ ] Front of house: tables, chairs, banquettes, decor, lighting, plants
- [ ] Tableware, glassware, cutlery, linen, napkins, menus, condiments, trays
- [ ] Bar equipment, glass washer, ice machine, coffee machine (grinder calibrated)
- [ ] Back-up equipment/spare parts list for critical items
- [ ] Equipment cleaning schedules and manuals filed

## 7. Technology & Systems (2–3 months out)
- [ ] POS system selected, configured with menu, modifiers, prices, tax, tipping, discounts
- [ ] Kitchen display system or printers tested end-to-end
- [ ] 🇮🇳 UPI QR codes, card/POS terminals tested; settlement account linked; GST-compliant invoicing
- [ ] Reservation/waitlist system (online + phone)
- [ ] 🇮🇳 Zomato / Swiggy listings (FSSAI number required) and online ordering set up and tested
- [ ] Inventory, accounting, payroll and scheduling software connected
- [ ] Reliable Wi-Fi/network, backup internet, CCTV and access control
- [ ] Music/audio licence and sound system tested
- [ ] Data backup, user permissions, cash-handling and void/discount controls set
- [ ] Gift cards/loyalty programme if offered

## 8. Staffing & Training (2–3 months out)
- [ ] Organisation chart and role descriptions (GM, chef, supervisors, FOH, BOH, stewarding, cleaners)
- [ ] Labour budget and weekly rota template
- [ ] Recruitment: job posts, interviews, trade tests (cook tests, service trials), reference checks
- [ ] 🇮🇳 ID and address proof, police verification (especially for migrant staff), Aadhaar/PAN, bank accounts, appointment letters, PF/ESI enrolment, payroll set up
- [ ] 🇮🇳 FSSAI FoSTaC training: Food Safety Supervisor certificate (at least one per 25 food handlers) and training records for all handlers
- [ ] 🇮🇳 Annual medical fitness certificates and typhoid vaccination for all food handlers (FSSAI Schedule 4)
- [ ] Employee handbook: dress code, grooming, hygiene, attendance, conduct, tips, grievance
- [ ] Onboarding, uniforms and name badges issued
- [ ] Training programme: menu knowledge, allergen handling, service steps, POS, upselling, complaints, safety, emergency procedures
- [ ] Alcohol service/responsible-service training (if applicable)
- [ ] First-aid trained staff and fire wardens appointed
- [ ] Service standards and "opening/closing" duty checklists written
- [ ] Staff meal policy and break schedule

## 9. Food Safety, Health & Safety (2 months out; must be complete before opening)
- [ ] HACCP plan written, critical control points and limits defined
- [ ] Food safety management records: temperature logs (fridges, freezers, hot-hold, cooking, cooling), cleaning logs, delivery logs, probe calibration
- [ ] Colour-coding system for boards, knives, cloths
- [ ] Allergen control procedure and cross-contact prevention
- [ ] Shelf-life/date-labelling policy; waste and disposal procedure
- [ ] Cleaning and sanitising schedules and chemical dilution chart; chemical storage and safety data sheets
- [ ] Pest-control contract with scheduled visits and logs
- [ ] Water test / filter checks; ice machine cleaning schedule
- [ ] Staff illness-reporting policy and exclusion rules
- [ ] First-aid kits, burn kits, eyewash, accident book
- [ ] Fire extinguishers, fire blankets, alarms, emergency lighting, exit signage, evacuation plan and drill completed
- [ ] Gas safety/leak detection and electrical safety inspections
- [ ] Risk assessments (kitchen, slips/trips, manual handling, hot oils, knives)
- [ ] 🇮🇳 Mock inspection against the FSSAI Schedule 4 hygiene checklist; deficiencies corrected
- [ ] 🇮🇳 FSSAI/Food Safety Officer, fire and excise inspections passed; licences and certificates displayed

## 10. Branding, Marketing & Launch (6 weeks to launch)
- [ ] Brand identity: name, logo, colours, tone of voice, signage, uniforms, packaging
- [ ] Website with menu, hours, location, reservations, contact, Google/Apple Maps pins
- [ ] Google Business Profile, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, WhatsApp Business set up
- [ ] Professional food and interior photography/video
- [ ] Pre-launch teaser campaign and email/WhatsApp waitlist
- [ ] PR: press release, food bloggers/influencers, media tasting invitations
- [ ] Local outreach: neighbouring offices, hotels, residential communities
- [ ] Opening offer / launch promotion defined and tracked
- [ ] Review-management plan (respond to every review)
- [ ] Delivery-app photos, descriptions and promo set
- [ ] Marketing budget and KPI tracking

## 11. Front-of-House Readiness (final 2 weeks)
- [ ] Table layout, spacing and numbering set; reservations book loaded
- [ ] Signage, menu boards, opening hours, licence displayed
- [ ] Lighting, music volume and temperature tested at service times
- [ ] Toilets stocked and inspected; spare consumables ready
- [ ] Float cash, change, receipts roll and spare card-terminal paper
- [ ] Lost-property, complaints, refunds and comps procedures
- [ ] Service sequence rehearsed: greeting, seating, order taking, delivery, payment, farewell
- [ ] Accessible seating, high chairs, booster seats

## 12. Kitchen Readiness (final 2 weeks)
- [ ] Mise-en-place lists, prep schedules and par levels per station
- [ ] Every dish plated to spec and photographed for reference
- [ ] Ticket times and kitchen flow tested at volume
- [ ] Equipment commissioned; temperatures verified; spare gas/fuel confirmed
- [ ] Stock rotated, labelled and stored correctly
- [ ] Dish pit staffed and tested with a full load

## 13. Trial Runs & Soft Opening (1–2 weeks)
- [ ] Dry run (staff only) — full menu, POS, kitchen, bar, payments
- [ ] Friends & family service with feedback forms
- [ ] Reduced-capacity soft opening / limited menu for several days
- [ ] Debrief after each service; fix list tracked and closed
- [ ] Menu items adjusted or removed based on feedback and kitchen performance
- [ ] Final go/no-go meeting with owner, GM and head chef

## 14. Opening Day
- [ ] Morning briefing: covers, reservations, specials, 86'd items, allergens, VIPs
- [ ] Opening checklist (lights, AC, equipment on, temps logged, handwash stocked, floats counted)
- [ ] POS, printers, terminals, Wi-Fi tested
- [ ] Management on the floor all day; owner/GM on call
- [ ] Photographer, social media posting and live updates
- [ ] Guest feedback captured; issues logged in real time
- [ ] End-of-day: cash-up, sales report, stock/waste log, closing checklist, security lock-up, debrief

## 15. First 30–90 Days After Opening
- [ ] Weekly flash P&L: sales, food cost, labour cost, waste
- [ ] Weekly stocktake and variance review
- [ ] Menu engineering review; remove poor performers
- [ ] Review online ratings and guest comments weekly
- [ ] Staff performance reviews, re-training and rota optimisation
- [ ] Supplier performance and price review
- [ ] Equipment maintenance schedule running; service logs updated
- [ ] Compliance re-check: licences, certificates, training renewals and expiry dates
- [ ] Marketing retargeting, loyalty and repeat-customer tracking
- [ ] 90-day review versus business plan; adjust forecasts

## 16. Lighting Plan (3–4 months out; test before soft opening)
- [ ] Lighting designer or electrician appointed; lighting plan drawn for each zone (dining, bar, kitchen, passages, washroom, outdoor)
- [ ] Target light levels set: dining about 50–150 lux at the table, bar 100–200, kitchen about 500–550 (confirm FSSAI figure), passages 200–300, outdoor paths 10–50
- [ ] Colour temperature chosen: 2200–2700 K warm in dining and bar, 4000–5000 K in kitchen and back of house
- [ ] Fittings specified with CRI 90+ in dining, CRI 80+ in back of house
- [ ] Dimmable, low-flicker LED drivers (0–10 V or DALI) fitted on all dining and bar circuits
- [ ] Lighting scenes programmed and labelled: lunch, sunset, evening, late
- [ ] Glare checked from every seat; fittings shaded or aimed away from guests' eyes
- [ ] Accent light on the gallery wall, bar back and focal pieces
- [ ] Outdoor and open-edge fittings rated IP44 or higher (IP65 where rain can reach), corrosion-resistant for the coast
- [ ] Emergency lighting, exit signs and critical circuits on backup power
- [ ] Lux test on site at dusk with a lux meter and a printed menu; adjust and record readings
- [ ] Spare lamps and drivers stocked; cleaning and replacement schedule set

## 17. Mosquito & Pest Control (before soft opening, then monthly)
- [ ] No standing water: planters, pebble beds, saucers, gutters, drains, grease trap, ice bins and buckets checked and drained
- [ ] Floor drains and gutters sloped to empty fully; vents and drain openings fitted with fine mesh
- [ ] Planting near seating pruned and well drained so it does not hold damp or give mosquitoes resting spots
- [ ] Licensed pest-control contractor appointed with monthly larvicide and outdoor treatment (also covers kitchen pest control; records kept)
- [ ] Panchayat fogging and larvicide schedule checked and coordinated
- [ ] Low-level fans at table height along the open edges, plus the ceiling fans
- [ ] Warm amber lighting on the open sides; no bright white floodlights or UV on the guest side; any trap or zapper kept well away from seating and food
- [ ] Repellent basket or spray at the entrance for guests
- [ ] PVC curtains lowered during the dusk peak, with the fans running
- [ ] Fine mosquito netting or fly screens on kitchen, bar and store openings; self-closing doors or an air curtain at the kitchen
- [ ] No fogging or chemical spraying while guests are present or food is exposed; treat only before opening or after closing with approved products
- [ ] Monsoon routine: more frequent drain checks, treatment and curtain use

- Never open before all regulatory approvals and certificates are in hand.
- Keep a master folder (digital + printed) with licences, leases, contracts, insurance, inspection reports and warranties.
- Assign an owner and due date to every item above.
- Review this list weekly in the last 8 weeks before opening.
