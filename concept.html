    How the pictures were made. Each frame was taken from the 3D model at eye level (1.6 m) in the evening lighting setting, then turned into a photograph-like render in Google Flow (project “Garden Concept Renders”) with instructions to keep the camera, layout and every object unchanged, and upscaled to 2K. Each one was checked at full size against its model frame for added, missing or moved objects, and any stray or misspelled lettering the generator invented was removed by hand before it was published. They are concept images: small details such as plates, glassware and the exact plant shapes are illustrative, and most wall signs and posters are shown blank or with pictures only, as placeholders for the final artwork.
