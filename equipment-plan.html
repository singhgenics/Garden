Equipment plan

What goes where on opening day: the kitchen, the dish wash and the bar. Built from the menu (59 items) and the positions in the 3D model. Numbers on the plans match the schedules below each plan.

Where the three areas sit

Plan view, north up. The bar is at the east end of the hall; the kitchen block is across the 1.60 m pathway.

1 · Kitchen

5.41 × 4.38 m wall to wall · 5.21 × 4.18 m clear ·

Hot line. The four cooking units total 3.82 m on the east wall, under one hood. That leaves 0.36 m of the 4.18 m wall free. The island leaves 1.08 m of aisle on each side, and 1.36 m between the island and the cooks.

No oven, grill or tandoor

Nothing on the menu is baked or grilled. Pork belly braises in a pot on the stock range, bao is steamed, and everything else is wok, fryer or steamer. A combi oven is a later purchase if you want to batch the braise.

2 · Dish wash

5.41 × 2.00 m wall to wall · 5.21 × 1.80 m clear ·

Flow. Dirty ware comes in at the west door, goes through the receiving table, the sink and the hood dishwasher, and ends on the clean table. The wall run totals 5.17 m of 5.21 m, so there is no spare length. A pot-wash bowl would have to replace part of the clean table.

The wall between the dish wash and the kitchen has a shuttered window over the granite platform (at 2.3 to 3.3 m from the west wall), which lines up with the sink and dishwasher. A clean-ware hatch at the clean table (3.9 to 5.2 m) would save the walk round through the passage. That is a suggestion, not in the model.

3 · Bar

Back bar 2.80 m · counter 2.80 m · coffee station 1.30 m

Fix needed on the bar depth. In the 3D model the back bar is 0.42 m deep and the counter's working face is 1.00 m from the east wall, so the bartender aisle is only 0.58 m. A working bar needs about 0.90 m, and the under-counter machines are 0.60 m deep. This plan puts the counter's working face 1.50 m from the wall (0.50 m further into the hall): back bar 0.60 m, aisle 0.90 m, counter stand 0.60 m. Measure the real counter on site before ordering. Check that the move does not clash with the nearest tables.

If the counter cannot move, drop the glass washer from the bar and run glasses through the hood dishwasher, and use a slimline bottle cooler that is no more than 0.42 m deep.

6 · What is not verified

  • The kitchen depth (4.38 m), the dish-wash depth (2.00 m) and the wall heights are estimates in the model. Measure them on site.
  • Equipment sizes are typical Indian market sizes, plus or minus 0.05 m by brand. Get the vendor's drawing before you order each unit.
  • The hood airflow, the electrical load and the gas quantity are estimates. Have the HVAC vendor, an electrician and the gas supplier confirm them.
  • FSSAI, fire NOC, panchayat and LPG storage rules for Goa have not been checked. An advocate or consultant should confirm them.
  • The store-room door on the kitchen's south wall opens 3.47 to 4.37 m from the west wall. Check its swing against the hot-line end.
