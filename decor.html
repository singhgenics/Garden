Cabin East · Garden · decor concept

A green room in a Goan garden

A dark, warm, premium look for the Garden at night, in the spirit of the inspiration boards: low pools of brass and candle light, deep green, polished teak and lush planting. It is built on the updated 3D model of the site as it stands: the blue columns and navy balustrade, the white fretwork and fleur-de-lis eaves, the coffered white ceiling and the brick bar all stay. The new layer is a two-tone wall (bottle green over deep teal-green panelling that hides the old tiles), warm brass and jute light, walnut and cane chairs with cognac seats, burgundy linen on the tables, a garden-style bar with a coffee station and a fireplace under the TV, and a lit path from the gate to the door. Every render keeps the model's layout; only the finishes change.

Materials board

take this to suppliers

Built for Goa

The hall is open on three sides, so everything here gets monsoon humidity and salt air.

  • Wainscot: WPC or PVC board, not MDF, fixed on treated battens with a 10 mm air gap over the tiles, so damp behind it can dry out.
  • Paint: exterior-grade anti-fungal emulsion on the green wall even though it is inside.
  • Brick: a breathable matt sealer on the bar so spills wipe off without darkening the brick.
  • Brass: lacquered or powder-coated brass-finish fittings, or they go green within one monsoon.
  • Outdoor lights: every lantern and globe on the approach path IP65, on its own circuit with a timer.
  • Hanging planters: drip trays, and a hook rated for at least four times the wet weight.
  • Jute and cane: fine under the roof; outdoor jute globes should be the synthetic weather-proof kind.

Next steps

  1. Get a quote for the WPC wainscot (about 18 m run, 1 m high) and a sample panel painted in the deep teal-green.
  2. Paint sample boards of the bottle green and the teal-green side by side on site and look at them at night.
  3. Remove the tan ply from the bar and check the brick before sealing.
  4. Confirm the approach-path length and the balcony roof fixing points for the festoon canopy.
  5. Plan the coffee station: a water line for the brewer and urn, two 16 A sockets, and a drain if the urn is plumbed in.
  6. Order chair samples: walnut frame with cane back and a cognac vinyl seat, to check against the burgundy cloths.
  7. Plan lighting circuits: hall, bar, approach path and gate on separate dimmers or timers.
