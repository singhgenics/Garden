Cabin East · Garden · decor concept

A green room in a Goan garden

Dim sum and craft beer in the open hall of an old Goan house, photographed from every angle at blue hour. Built on the 3D model of the site as it stands: the blue columns, navy balustrade, white fretwork, coffered ceiling and brick bar stay. The new layer is bottle green over brown tile, a gallery wall that tells the story of Indo-Chinese food, ivy wicker orbs, bougainvillea and fairy lights.

Built for Goa

The hall is open on three sides, so everything gets monsoon humidity and salt air.

  • Tile: real glazed tile over the old tiles with a good adhesive; no foam stickers (they dent, lift in humidity and are combustible).
  • Paint: exterior-grade anti-fungal emulsion on the green wall even though it is inside.
  • Floor: honed anti-slip finish and a coir mat at the entrance step for the monsoon.
  • Brass: lacquered or powder-coated, or it goes green within one monsoon.
  • Orbs, cane, flowers: synthetic weather-proof rattan and ivy outside; seal natural cane and dried pieces inside.
  • Bougainvillea: full sun, pots or beds at the column bases, trained on stainless wire, thorns kept above head height.
  • Frames: sealed backs, prints behind glass, nothing unframed.
  • Mood: warm 2200 K on dimmers, a candle on every table, low 60s Cantopop, old Hindi and lounge jazz, one jasmine scent at the entrance, staff in bottle-green mandarin-collar shirts with a small brass pin.

Next steps

  1. Commission the gallery: paint or commission the bowl-and-thali oil still life for the statement spot, source prints (calendar girl, woodblock, ink-brush, Asian lager adverts), have a sign painter do the bilingual boards and menu card.
  2. Brief a muralist for the coffee-wall portrait and the washroom doodles.
  3. Tile and paint samples: the brown stacked tile and the bottle green side by side, seen at night.
  4. Order samples of the wicker orb, the walnut-and-cane chair with a cognac seat, and the burgundy cloth.
  5. Lighting circuits: orbs, gallery wash, bar, outdoor (fairy lights, posts, uplights) on separate dimmers or timers.
  6. Plant the bougainvillea now; it takes a season to cover the eave.
