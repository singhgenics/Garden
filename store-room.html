Store room

What the store room looks like when it is stocked: the whole room from above, down the aisle from both ends, and each rack head-on. The yellow dimension lines are the real sizes in metres. The images are illustrative renders made from the 3D model's layout; the sizes and positions come from the plan, and the stock and labels on the shelves are only examples.

2.70 × 6.46 m wall to wall · 2.50 × 6.26 m clear · racks 1.50 × 0.50 × 1.80 m with four shelves · aisle 1.46 m · door from the kitchen at the north end

The renders were made before the November site re-measure. The kitchen door into the store has moved about 0.27 m west (now 0.59 to 1.49 m from the west wall, reached through the 1.95 m passage in the kitchen's south-east corner); the racks and sizes are unchanged.

Shelf heights. Each rack has four shelves at 0.18, 0.68, 1.18 and 1.68 m from the floor, 0.50 m apart, with about 0.45 m clear above each shelf. Heavy tins, sacks and cases go on the bottom shelf, bottles and jars above. Keep everything 10 cm off the floor and 5 cm off the walls.
Where it comes from. Layout and sizes are from the equipment plan (section 4); what goes on each rack and how it rotates is in the stock plan. The renders were made in Google Flow (project “Store Room”) and checked against the layout; stock labels in them are not real brands.
Dimensions in metres. Not to scale: the pictures are perspective renders, so the lines mark each size, not a measuring scale.
